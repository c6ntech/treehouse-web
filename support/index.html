客服支援

樹屋 Treehouse 官方客服

聯絡我們

使用樹屋時遇到任何問題、想提供建議，或需要協助，歡迎寄信給我們的官方客服：

  • 客服信箱：support@cherryontech.com
  • 營運公司：櫻桃科技有限公司（Cherryon CO., LTD.）

我們收到來信後會盡快回覆。

來信時請提供

為了讓我們更快協助你，來信時請盡量附上：

  • 你的樹屋暱稱，以及註冊／登入所使用的方式（Apple 或 Google）
  • 手機型號與作業系統版本（例如 iPhone 15、iOS 18）
  • App 版本（可於 App 設定頁查看）
  • 問題的詳細描述、發生時間，以及相關截圖
為保護你的帳號安全，請不要在信中提供密碼或完整的付款卡號。

常見協助項目

以下問題在《常見問題》已有詳細說明，建議先參閱，通常能更快解決：

  • 訂閱與付費（試用、取消訂閱、恢復購買）：請見《常見問題－訂閱與付費》。
  • 安全與檢舉（檢舉、解除配對、封鎖）：請見《常見問題－安全與檢舉》。
  • 帳號管理（修改資料、綁定登入方式）：請見《常見問題－帳號管理》。
  • 刪除帳戶：請見《刪除帳戶》。

若仍無法解決，歡迎直接來信，我們會協助你。

退款申請

樹屋的訂閱由 Apple／Google 收取，退款需向對應平台申請。iOS 使用者請前往 reportaproblem.apple.com 提出申請。

Support

Treehouse Official Customer Support

Contact Us

If you run into any issue, have feedback, or need help with Treehouse, please email our official support team:

  • Support email: support@cherryontech.com
  • Operated by: Cherryon CO., LTD.

We'll get back to you as soon as possible.

What to Include

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:

  • Your Treehouse nickname and how you sign in (Apple or Google)
  • Your device model and OS version (e.g., iPhone 15, iOS 18)
  • The app version (shown in the app's Settings)
  • A detailed description of the issue, when it happened, and any screenshots
To keep your account safe, please never send your password or full payment card number by email.

Common Topics

These topics are covered in detail in our FAQ, which is usually the fastest way to get an answer:

  • Subscription & payment (free trial, canceling, restoring purchases): see FAQ – Subscription and Payment.
  • Safety & reporting (reporting, unmatching, blocking): see FAQ – Safety and Reporting.
  • Account management (editing your profile, linking sign-in methods): see FAQ – Account Management.
  • Delete account: see Delete Account.

If that doesn't solve it, just email us and we'll help.

Refunds

Treehouse subscriptions are billed by Apple / Google, so refunds must be requested through the corresponding platform. On iOS, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
