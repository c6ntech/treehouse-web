兒少性剝削與虐待防範聲明

最後更新日期：2026年7月

Treehouse 樹屋（櫻桃科技 Cherryon Co., Ltd. 旗下產品）致力於打造一個安全、健康的交友聊天環境，並嚴格遵守兒童安全相關法律規範。

我們的立場

Treehouse 對任何形式的兒少性剝削與虐待（CSAE, Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation）採取零容忍態度，包括但不限於：對兒童進行誘拐、性勒索、性剝削，或散布、儲存、分享任何兒少性虐待內容（CSAM, Child Sexual Abuse Material）。

我們的防範措施

  • 年齡限制：Treehouse 僅供年滿 18 歲之成年使用者註冊與使用。
  • 使用者檢舉機制：App 內建檢舉功能，使用者可隨時針對任何違規內容或行為提出檢舉，我們的團隊會儘速處理。
  • 內容處置：一經發現任何 CSAM 相關內容，我們將立即移除，並保存必要紀錄以配合主管機關調查。
  • 法規遵循：我們遵守台灣及國際相關兒童保護法律，並於發現違法情事時，主動向轄區主管機關（如警政單位）通報。

如何回報疑慮

若您在使用 Treehouse 時發現任何令人擔憂的內容或行為，請透過 App 內的檢舉功能，或電郵至 support@cherryontech.com 與我們聯繫。我們會認真看待每一則回報。

Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Prevention Statement

Last updated: July 2026

Treehouse (a product of Cherryon Co., Ltd.) is committed to providing a safe environment for chat-based social connection, and complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations.

Our Position

Treehouse maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including but not limited to grooming, sextortion, and sexual exploitation of minors, as well as the distribution, storage, or sharing of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) in any form.

Our Safeguards

  • Age Restriction: Treehouse is intended for and restricted to adult users aged 18 and above.
  • In-App Reporting: Users can report any content or behavior that violates our standards directly within the app at any time. Our team reviews all reports promptly.
  • Content Handling: Upon identifying any CSAM-related content, we take immediate action to remove it and retain necessary records to support law enforcement investigations.
  • Legal Compliance: We comply with applicable child protection laws in Taiwan and internationally, and proactively report violations to relevant authorities (including local law enforcement) when identified.

How to Report a Concern

If you encounter any concerning content or behavior while using Treehouse, please report it through the in-app reporting feature, or contact us at support@cherryontech.com. Every report is taken seriously.
